My contribution to this work focused on the development and application of a novel personalized lumbar spinal cord BOLD fMRI approach designed to map the segmental distribution of muscle innervation on an individual basis, using passive limb mobilization and muscle tendon mechanical vibration. I was also involved in constructing personalized models based on MRI and CT imaging of the spinal cord. These models were essential for conducting computational simulations to determine the optimal placement of EES implants for each participant.
