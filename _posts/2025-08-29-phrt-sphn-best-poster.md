I am thrilled to share that our poster won the **Best Poster Prize** at the [PHRT/SPHN From Technology to Treatment](https://www.sfa-phrt.ch/) conference (August 2025), shared with my colleague **Philippe Forero**.

**Poster:** *Digital Twin Framework for Personalized Epidural Electrical Stimulation in Spinal Cord Injury Therapies*

Philippe Forero, **Sergio Daniel Hernandez-Charpak**, Javier García Ordoñez, Guolei Sun, Oleg Bakhteev, Leonid Iosipoi, Alice Bruel, Mathieu Salzmann, Tianfei Zhou, Taylor Newton, Yu Qin, Remy Tang, Hanane Moha-Ouchane, Grégory Dumont, Jean-Baptiste Ledoux, Fabio Becce, Gaia Carparelli, Pedro Abranches, Eduardo Martin Maraud, Léonie Asboth, Robin Demesmaeker, Tomislav Milekovic,  Ender Konukoglu, Guillaume Obozinski, Niels Kuster, Esra Neufeld,  Henri Lorach, Jocelyne Bloch and Gregoire Courtine

---

Our poster presented our current framework for building personalized digital twins of spinal cord injury (SCI) individuals, integrating medical imaging-derived models of the spinal cord, the spinal circuits, and the musculoskeletal body. By combining these complementary modelling layers into a unified, patient-specific framework, we aim to capture the full anatomical and functional complexity relevant to therapeutic interventions.

We demonstrated the application of this complete framework in the context of clinical trials exploring epidural electrical stimulation (EES) in SCI individuals — showing how personalized models can guide implantation, inform stimulation parameters, and ultimately help translate neurotechnology from the lab to the clinic.
